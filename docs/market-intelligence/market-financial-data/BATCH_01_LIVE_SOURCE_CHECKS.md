## Interpretation

- Codal is the primary source of record for these company reports. The direct Codal report pages expose report dates, audit/consolidation state, units, and original table columns.
- yfinance-ir successfully parsed and source-matched the sampled financial statement rows for فایرا, شکبیر, کاریز, and شپدیس. It matched selected monthly source cells for فایرا and the current-month total for کاریز, but its generic monthly DataFrame loses measure headings and aggregation basis. For کاریز it places the current-month amount beside a prior cumulative amount; for شکبیر and شپدیس it selects count fields where the source also has sales amounts. Do not accept the generic monthly output as a normalized monthly series without retaining and interpreting the raw table dimensions.
- برانسفو supplies an older financial report, but its pre-1397 monthly report could not be downloaded due timeout/rate limiting. It has no recent 1400–1405 financial or monthly letter in the queried period. This report needs a later retry after Codal's cooldown.
- These checks do not complete the requested historical-window matrix (1397–1398 and 1400–1402), CodalPy coverage, or full report-type comparison. No provider has passed the acceptance gate and no implementation decision should be made from this sample alone.
